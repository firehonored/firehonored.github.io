FIRELORD ZUKO

Zuko is the eldest child of Ozai and Ursa. His younger sister is Azula. He grew up in the Fire Nation as its' Prince. Even though he didn't often side with the ideas his father had for the nation, he wanted his approval anyway. Getting banished was one of the worst things to ever happen to the young prince.

He spent three long years searching for the Avatar, with his Uncle Iroh at his side. Zuko was impatient, broody and quick to temper, but Iroh was ever patient with him. When Zuko finally did find and fail to capture Avatar Aang, Iroh was the one to help him realize that maybe capturing the Avatar was not his destiny after all.

A change overtook the prince as he realized that things were not set in stone. His family was wrong. His father was wrong. Deep down he always knew it, his father had made his mother disappear, he had scarred him permanently for life and now Ozai wanted to take over the world. *Zuko could not allow that to happen*.

He ended up joining ranks with the Avatar and friends. even going so far as to help teach Aang how to firebend. It took a minute for them all to trust him and with good reason. Then again, they did not know him as well as they had assumed he was. Zuko could be awkward, but lovable, warm and kind. And when given the chance, he is a great tutor and friend.

During the final battle, Zuko takes on his sister. He defends Katara when his sister launches a lightning attack on her, resulting in another scar that lingers at his middle for the rest of his life. Aang ends the war and it is time to rebuild.
